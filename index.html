LinkedIn accounts & daily limits

Add the LinkedIn profile(s) you send from. No password is stored here – you stay logged in to LinkedIn in your own browser and send each message yourself. This keeps your account safe (LinkedIn restricts automated tools).

Safe range for a new account: 20–30 requests/day and under ~100 per week. Increase slowly once your accept rate is above 30%.

All data is saved only in this browser. Download a backup regularly.
